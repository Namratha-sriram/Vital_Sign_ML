Clinical Safety & Validation Methodology

SAFETY PROTOCOL

False Negative Minimization

In medical informatics, False Negatives carry high clinical cost because an undetected deteriorating patient is discharged prematurely. The Random Forest model lowers FN from 12.4% (Linear models) down to 6.1%.

VALIDATION DESIGN

Stratified 5-Fold Cross Validation

To eliminate training bias, 50,000 records were partitioned into 5 stratified folds maintaining a 23.7% readmission ratio in each fold, yielding a standard deviation of <±0.8% across splits.

DEPLOYMENT READINESS

PyCharm & Kaggle Reproducibility

The entire pipeline executes deterministically via python src/train_models.py with random_state=42, persisting validated weights in the local models/ directory.
